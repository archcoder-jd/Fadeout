Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

In short: Fadeout has no accounts, sets no cookies of its own, and uses no analytics or advertising. Some limited technical information is handled by our server and by the third-party services that provide movie data, fonts and trailers, as explained below.

1. Who we are

Fadeout (fadeout.venture31.com) is a movie and TV discovery website operated by Venture31, a web development business based in Ontario, Canada. In this policy, "we," "us" and "our" mean Venture31.

2. Information we handle

Server logs

Like almost every website, our hosting server automatically records basic technical information when you visit, including your IP address, browser type, the pages and files requested, and the date and time. We use these logs only to keep the site secure, prevent abuse and fix problems. They are kept for a limited period and then deleted.

Searches and browsing within Fadeout

When you browse trending titles, search, or open a title's details, your browser sends that request to our server, which forwards it to The Movie Database (TMDB) to get the results. Because search terms are part of the request, they may appear in our server logs. We do not use them for any other purpose, link them to you, or share them with anyone other than TMDB as needed to answer the request.

What we don't collect

We do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details, and we do not create profiles of visitors. We do not sell or rent any information.

3. Cookies and local storage

Fadeout itself does not set cookies or store data in your browser. Some third-party services used on the site may set their own cookies, as described in the next section.

4. Third-party services

Fadeout relies on the following services. When your browser loads content from them, they receive technical information such as your IP address, and they handle it under their own privacy policies.

  • The Movie Database (TMDB) provides movie and TV information and poster images. TMDB privacy policy
  • YouTube hosts the trailers shown in title details. YouTube may set cookies when a trailer loads or plays. Google privacy policy
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces used on the site. Google privacy policy
  • Image hosts, including Amazon and IMDb, serve some of the artwork shown on the site.

Links to other websites

Fadeout links to other websites, such as streaming services listed in Watch for Free. Once you follow a link, that website's own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for how other websites handle your information.

5. Children

Fadeout is a general-audience website and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law, you can ask what personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Since Fadeout does not collect personal details, this would normally only apply to information in server logs. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how Fadeout handles information, for example by adding new features, we will update this page and its effective date.
